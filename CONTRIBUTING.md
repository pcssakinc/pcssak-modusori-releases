# Contribution and issue conduct

This is a binary distribution and issue-tracking repository, not the application source
repository. Source-code pull requests are not accepted here.

Before opening an issue, search existing reports and verify the behavior on the newest official
release. Submit one reproducible problem or one user need per issue. AI-assisted reports are
welcome only when the reporter has personally checked every claim and attachment.

Be respectful, avoid personal or confidential information, and do not post generated issue spam,
security exploits, licence keys, or files you do not have the right to share. Maintainers may edit,
close, lock, or remove material that is unsafe, duplicated, unverifiable, or outside this
repository's scope.
